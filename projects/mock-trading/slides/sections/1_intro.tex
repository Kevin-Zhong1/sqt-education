\begin{frame}{What is Quantitative Finance?}
  \begin{sqt:definition}
    \textbf{Quantitative finance} is the use of mathematical models, statistics, data,
    and algorithms to make trading decisions.
  \end{sqt:definition}

  \begin{itemize}
    \item Quantitative finance is a broad term, encompassing a wide range of activities and roles.
    \item Different types of firms in the trading industry incorporate quantitative finance.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Why the Trading Industry?}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Intellectually challenging
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Fast paced environment, complex and ambiguous problems, interdisciplinary work
    \end{itemize}
    \item High agency and ownership
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Smaller teams, flatter hierarchies, less bureaucracy
    \end{itemize}
    \item Benefit to society
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Liquidity provision, making markets more efficient, price discovery
    \end{itemize}
    \item High compensation
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
